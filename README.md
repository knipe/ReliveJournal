# ReliveJournal
A nostalgic, early-2000s LiveJournal recreation hosted by yours truly. Featuring classic [entries], [userinfo], and [calendar] navigation wrapped in a bold Pink Panther retro layout, it brings back the charm of personal, unfiltered internet journaling complete with long-form diary reflections, daily updates, and zero algorithms.

## The Story

In January 2000, at just 10 years old, I joined a website called LiveJournal. I chose my username after my favorite Fleetwood Mac song, Gypsy:

> So I'm back, to the velvet underground /
> Back to the floor, that I love /
> To a room with some lace and paper flowers /
> Back to the gypsy that I was /
> To the gypsy... that I was 

Little did I know that joining that site would spark a habit of writing a personal diary entry every single day for the last 25+ years. 

After LiveJournal was acquired by Russian ownership in 2009, the platform began to change. Over the years, I migrated my diary across the internet—first to Tumblr, then to WordPress, and most recently to Dreamwidth. While each platform had its merits, none of them ever felt quite the same. 

So, I went back to the roots of the early web, took the classic LiveJournal layout and code, and built my own version.

## Features

* Classic Early-2000s UI: Faithful `[entries]`, `[userinfo]`, and `[calendar]` navigation wrapped in a bold, high-contrast retro Pink Panther theme.
* Distraction-Free Writing: Designed purely for long-form, chronological diary entries—no feeds, no likes, no algorithms.
* Lightweight & Indie-Web Ready: Static-friendly architecture hosted on Neocities (`homedifferently.neocities.org`)[cite: 1].
